# Community expectations

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical decisions with evidence, give useful feedback, and allow others to correct mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publication of private information, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community.

Contact the maintainers privately about sensitive incidents when a private channel is available. Otherwise, open an issue requesting a private contact without repeating abusive or private material. These expectations apply to this repository's issues, discussions, pull requests, and other project spaces.
